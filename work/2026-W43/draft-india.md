## Sarvam and the Indian labs

Chariot, which AIM calls a frontier AI research lab, launched Chariot-1-ASR Realtime on 22 Oct to transcribe Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia and Indian English while people are still talking; it can identify the language itself, write borrowed English words in either script, and is available on the Chariot platform [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/chariot-launches-speech-recognition-model-for-10-indian-languages).

The results are from Chariot's own tests: on 79 public test sets covering around 153,000 recordings, it reports 26% fewer errors than Google Gemini 3.5 Transcribe and 9% fewer than Saaras v4 among whole-recording systems, and 17% fewer than Saaras v4 and 57% fewer than ElevenLabs Scribe v2 Realtime among streaming ones, where Chariot was not told the language and rivals got the language settings that worked best for them [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/chariot-launches-speech-recognition-model-for-10-indian-languages).

On Vaani, Hindi recordings from speakers across different districts, Chariot reports a 17.21% error rate against 17.86% for Saaras v4, and on Monsoon's unscripted Hindi phone calls 5.04% against 8.27% for OpenAI's gpt-transcribe [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/chariot-launches-speech-recognition-model-for-10-indian-languages).

Saaras V4 itself supports 22 Indian languages and English, AIM noted in a separate 19 Oct feature on Indian voice AI [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-features/karnatakas-elevenlabs-partnership-irks-indian-voice-ai-startups).

Birla AI Labs, an AI initiative and venture studio under the Aditya Birla Group, released Yug-1, its first time series foundation model, to forecast outcomes, identify patterns and detect anomalies across interconnected datasets, and the company says its team took it from training to deployment in under six months [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/birla-ai-labs-launches-yug-1-a-time-series-foundation-model-built-in-india).

Founder and chairperson Ananya Birla says Yug-1 outperformed other first-generation time series models on the GiftEval benchmark and performed competitively against newer-generation ones, both claims from the lab itself, and the model is on GitHub and Hugging Face for developers and researchers to test [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/birla-ai-labs-launches-yug-1-a-time-series-foundation-model-built-in-india).

*Nothing verifiable from Sarvam, Krutrim, AI4Bharat or BharatGen themselves this week; I found no release of theirs dated 19 to 25 Oct.*

**My read:** Chariot's numbers deserve an outside rerun before anyone switches, and that rerun is a decent student project in itself. Yug-1 is the one to open this weekend, since a model you can download and test beats a leaderboard you have to trust.

## Government, compute and policy

*Nothing verifiable this week from MeitY, PIB or the IndiaAI Mission; I found nothing of theirs dated 19 to 25 Oct.*

In Karnataka, AIM reported on 19 Oct that ElevenLabs recently signed an MoU with the Karnataka Innovation and Technology Society to explore Kannada voice AI in citizen services, skilling and grievance redressal, including free lifetime licences for mute users to communicate through a synthetic version of their lost voice [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-features/karnatakas-elevenlabs-partnership-irks-indian-voice-ai-startups).

Indian voice AI founders pushed back in [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-features/karnatakas-elevenlabs-partnership-irks-indian-voice-ai-startups): "If the model sits abroad, India is renting intelligence, not building it," said Navana.ai chief executive Raoul Nanavati, though CoRover.ai chief executive Ankush Sabharwal cautioned that the risk is "preference becomes blind protection".

On compute, Utho Cloud opened reservations for NVIDIA B300 GPUs in India ahead of a planned February 2027 deployment, for one, two or three years, allocated in order of reservation, with rupee billing and India-hosted infrastructure; AIM gives no prices and no B300 count, though Utho has outlined plans to deploy 10,000 GPUs [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/utho-cloud-opens-b300-gpu-bookings-ahead-of-february-2027-rollout).

Cortex DC, the AI-first subsidiary of The Chatterjee Group, plans 2 GW of data centre capacity across India for hyperscalers, cloud providers and AI workloads, targets operational readiness for its first data centre within 18 months of first shovel and plans to source 51 per cent of its energy from green sources, but the report names no site, cost or customer [Indian Infrastructure](https://indianinfrastructure.com/2026/10/23/cortex-dc-advances-plans-to-develop-2-gw-of-ai-ready-data-centre-capacity-across-india/).

**My read:** None of this is compute a student rents by the hour: the B300s go out as one-to-three-year reservations and the Cortex report names no site. Karnataka is the thread to follow, because whoever wins state voice contracts is likely to be the one hiring for Indian-language speech work.

## Startups and money

AI led a slower funding week: Indian startups raised over $184 Mn across 17 deals between 19 and 23 Oct, down 22% from the week before, and AI startups took $93.9 Mn of it across six deals, all six listed under application layer in Inc42's table [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/from-even-health-to-the-pant-project-indian-startups-raised-over-184-mn-this-week/).

- **Different**, Ajeet Sing's AI martech startup, came out of stealth with a $48 Mn seed round led by General Catalyst, with Lightspeed Venture Partners, the biggest AI cheque in Inc42's tally of Indian startup rounds that week [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/from-even-health-to-the-pant-project-indian-startups-raised-over-184-mn-this-week/).
- **AuxoAI**, whose AI strategists and engineers work alongside client staff at companies such as UKG and Fogo de Chão, raised a $20.7 million Series A led by NTTVC [StartupTalky](https://startuptalky.com/news/daily-indian-funding-roundup-key-news-21-october-2026/).
- **Hunar.AI**, whose conversational agents handle candidate screening and interview scheduling for frontline employers in regional languages, raised Rs 39.3 crore in a Series A led by Blume Ventures, according to [Indian Startup News](https://indianstartupnews.com/funding/ai-hiring-startup-hunarai-raises-rs-393-crore-in-funding-led-by-blume-ventures-12690269), which cites reports based on regulatory filings; Inc42's table lists the round at $4.2 Mn [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/from-even-health-to-the-pant-project-indian-startups-raised-over-184-mn-this-week/).
- **Karta AI** raised a $3 million seed round for voice AI that automates customer interactions in banking, lending and insurance [Local Samosa](https://www.localsamosa.com/news/startup-roundup-fourth-week-of-october-2026-12740723).
- **Blacksmith**, a San Francisco-based AI code-testing startup, raised a $66 Mn Series C led by Sequoia Capital at a $1 Bn valuation, with Peak XV, which led its Series B in August, investing again [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/peak-xv-backed-ai-startup-blacksmith-nets-66-mn-at-a-unicorn-valuation/).

At the growth stage, F2A, anchored by Nandan Nilekani, has secured ₹3,000 crore in subscriptions for its AI and deeptech fund and plans cheques of ₹100 crore to ₹200 crore into 12-15 companies, about 40% of it for physical AI such as semiconductors and robotics [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-features/why-nandan-nilekani-backed-f2a-believes-indias-investment-opportunity-lies-in-edge-ai).

Nikhil Kamath expects money to get harder to raise: in an NDTV World Summit interview streamed on 22 Oct, he said maybe a trillion dollars a year is going into AI against $100 to $200 billion coming out through enterprises, and that raising a lot of capital today and keeping it is "probably not a bad idea" [Singju Post](https://singjupost.com/transcript-nikhil-kamath-interview-at-ndtv-world-summit-2026/).

"I think the odds are low," Kamath said in the same interview of an Indian company competing at the frontier-model layer; if he were 17 today, he would build on the application or services layers [Singju Post](https://singjupost.com/transcript-nikhil-kamath-interview-at-ndtv-world-summit-2026/). On Kamath's WTF podcast on 23 Oct, Turing's Vijay Krishnan counted about $700 billion committed to AI this year against roughly $100 billion of AI enterprise revenue [Scuttlebutt](https://getscuttlebutt.substack.com/p/turing-cto-not-one-fortune-500-ai).

**My read:** All six AI deals in Inc42's table went to companies selling AI into someone's workflow, which is the layer Kamath says he would build in. If you are choosing where to intern, a funded application-layer startup looks like a safer 2027 bet than waiting for an Indian frontier lab.

## AI in the wild in India

Infosys put a number on its AI business: AI revenue grew 29% quarter on quarter in the September quarter to 10.5% of the total, while quarterly revenue of $5.145 billion grew 1.2% sequentially in constant currency and the FY27 outlook was cut to 1%-2% from 1.5%-3% [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/infosys-ai-revenue-rises-29-in-q2-fy27-growth-guidance-cut).

Infosys also booked $5.2 billion in large deals, including a mega deal of over $2 billion, and is deploying Cognition's Devin AI software engineer across internal engineering operations and client engagements [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/infosys-ai-revenue-rises-29-in-q2-fy27-growth-guidance-cut).

In voice, India is ElevenLabs' second-largest market outside the US, with more than 250 businesses using its technology and 100 million conversations on ElevenAgents over the past year, more than 70% of them in Hindi, Kannada, Tamil and Telugu [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-features/karnatakas-elevenlabs-partnership-irks-indian-voice-ai-startups).

Razorpay, which partners with ElevenLabs, Gnani.ai and Sarvam AI, has deployed voice AI at scale in marketing, sales and risk, and its chief product officer Khilan Haria says 20–30% of all searches in India are voice [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-features/karnatakas-elevenlabs-partnership-irks-indian-voice-ai-startups).

**My read:** Infosys is the signal for campus hiring: its AI revenue is growing far faster than the company and Devin already sits in its engineering, so expect interviews to ask how you work with coding agents.

## Ideas for India

### An independent scoreboard for Indian speech

Chariot's launch figures all come from its own tests [AIM on Chariot](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/chariot-launches-speech-recognition-model-for-10-indian-languages), and Navana.ai's Raoul Nanavati says open evaluations and clear benchmarks could help ensure the best companies are chosen [AIM on Karnataka](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-features/karnatakas-elevenlabs-partnership-irks-indian-voice-ai-startups).

**My read:** State bodies like KITS and banks buying voice bots would pay for a neutral number before they sign. This semester, build a harness that runs any vendor's speech API on public Hindi sets such as Vaani and Monsoon and publishes error rates by accent and line quality.

### Forecasts small sellers can check

Yug-1 is on GitHub and Hugging Face, and Ananya Birla names demand and supply among the time-dependent data it is built for, writing on LinkedIn that "so much of the data that actually runs businesses and economies moves through time" [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/birla-ai-labs-launches-yug-1-a-time-series-foundation-model-built-in-india).

**My read:** Distributors and D2C brands that reorder by gut feel would pay for forecasts they can audit. Build a tool that takes a shop's sales export, runs Yug-1 against a seasonal-naive baseline per product, and shows the forecast only where it wins.

### Proof of where inference ran

CoRover.ai's Ankush Sabharwal counts a lack of transparency over where inference takes place among the risks at scale [AIM on Karnataka](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-features/karnatakas-elevenlabs-partnership-irks-indian-voice-ai-startups), and Kamath sees an opening for an Indian inference company that serves open-source models while companies keep their own context [Singju Post](https://singjupost.com/transcript-nikhil-kamath-interview-at-ndtv-world-summit-2026/).

**My read:** Banks, insurers and state departments that have to answer where citizen data went would pay for that proof. Build an OpenAI-compatible gateway that serves an open model from an Indian region and writes a signed log of where each request ran.
