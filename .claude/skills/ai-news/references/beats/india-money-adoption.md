# Beat: india-money-adoption (edition: india)

**What this beat covers.** Money and adoption in India. Money: funding rounds and acquisitions of Indian AI startups, what Nikhil Kamath's WTF Fund and WTF podcast are backing or saying, Peak XV, Accel India, Lightspeed India, Blume, Elevation, the big global funds' India deals, government grants with amounts. Adoption: AI actually in use at Indian companies and institutions with numbers (banks, Zomato/Swiggy/Zepto, Jio, Tata, Infosys/TCS/Wipro/HCL deals, hospitals, courts, railways, farms, schools, UPI/NPCI), AI-driven hiring or layoffs at Indian firms, OpenAI/Anthropic/Google/Microsoft moves in India (offices, pricing, free tiers, education deals, Go plans), Indian app launches that got traction.

**What counts.** A round with an amount and a named investor; an acquisition; a deployment with a number (users, branches, districts, crore saved); a pricing or availability change for India; a podcast episode only if the page states who said what (prefer the written coverage).

**What does not count.** Projections ("will add $X trillion"), surveys without method, press releases that only say "partnered to explore".
