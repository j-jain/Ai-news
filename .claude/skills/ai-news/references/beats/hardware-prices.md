# Beat: hardware-prices (edition: world)

**What this beat covers.** The hardware a student might buy or watch: DRAM and NAND contract and spot prices (TrendForce), DDR5 kit and SSD street prices, GPU prices and launches (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel), laptop launches and prices with an India price when a page states one, Apple / Qualcomm / AMD / Intel silicon for local AI, Mac memory configurations and prices, cloud GPU rental prices, India-specific pricing and availability (Indian retail pages, Flipkart/Amazon.in launch pages that state a price), supply news that moves prices (HBM demand, fab capacity, tariffs).

**What counts.** A TrendForce press release with the quarter's change; a retailer page that states a price today; a launch page with specs and price; a regulator's tariff notice; a vendor's spec page.

**What does not count.** Forum rumours, "leaker" posts, price screenshots on X, affiliate listicles without dates.
