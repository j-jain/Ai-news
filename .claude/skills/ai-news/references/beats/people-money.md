# Beat: people-money (edition: world)

**What this beat covers.** The people and the money that steer the field. People: Andrej Karpathy (blog, GitHub, talks with a transcript or write-up), Elon Musk and xAI (via fetchable coverage), Sam Altman, Dario Amodei, Demis Hassabis, Yann LeCun, Ilya Sutskever, Jensen Huang, Satya Nadella, Sundar Pichai, Mark Zuckerberg - when they publish, ship, hire, leave or testify. Money: Sequoia, a16z, YC (Requests for Startups, batch themes, Demo Day), Thrive, Founders Fund, SoftBank, sovereign funds; rounds above $100M; acquisitions and acqui-hires; IPO filings; public-company AI numbers from earnings releases.

**What counts.** The person's own post or repo; the firm's own essay or RFS page; a company's press release or SEC filing; a reputable outlet's report with figures. Talks only when a transcript, slides or a detailed write-up exists.

**What does not count.** Tweets as the sole source, podcast summaries by anonymous accounts, "insiders say" valuations.
