## If you only read three things

- **India is now ChatGPT's largest market** OpenAI India MD Prabhjeet Singh said India has become ChatGPT's largest market globally by weekly active users, with the local user base doubling since January 2026, and that OpenAI is now working on Indian-language capabilities and Android-first products [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/india-becomes-chatgpts-largest-market-globally-user-base-doubles-since-january/), so the product you use for coursework is being built around users like you.
- **Sarvam puts Prism's open weights online** Sarvam released Prism on 3 Nov, a decision model built on Sarvam 105B that it says scores 71.8 at 44 ms on a B200 GPU among the 25 decision models it tested, with weights on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0 [Sarvam](https://www.sarvam.ai/blogs/sarvam-prism), so you can fine-tune an Indian model on your own data without a licence argument.
- **IndiaAI unveils EKA-1 and two Dhirs** Soket's 26.3 billion parameter EKA-1 and ZenteiQ's Dhīr 2B and Dhīr 5B were unveiled under the IndiaAI Mission in New Delhi before IT secretary S Krishnan, and ZenteiQ released its weights under Apache 2.0 through AIKosh [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/tech/indiaai-mission-unveils-three-indigenous-ai-models-as-soket-launches-eka-1-and-zenteiq-releases-dhr-2b-and-5b), so there are now government-backed Indic bases you can download for a class project.

## Sarvam and the Indian labs

Prism is not a chatbot: Sarvam describes it as a LoRA fine-tune of Sarvam 105B that takes a state, a question and a set of options in a single forward pass, and a small scoring head returns a probability for each option with no token-by-token generation [Sarvam](https://www.sarvam.ai/blogs/sarvam-prism).

The headline score averages three indices, the Jev Decision Index of 37 public benchmarks, Sarvam's own recreation of the Artificial Analysis Decision Index and an internal Indian-language benchmark, and on that last one Prism scores 82.5 against 82.6 for KnowLine-35B, while mammoth-1 tops the average at 75.2 but answers in 284 ms [Sarvam](https://www.sarvam.ai/blogs/sarvam-prism).

Read the fine print before you quote it: Sarvam ran Prism and 24 other decision models through the three indices itself and timed every model it could on the same B200 hardware using the bundled inference code [Sarvam](https://www.sarvam.ai/blogs/sarvam-prism).

The calibration numbers are the interesting part: for Indian-language answers given with 90% confidence or more, Prism predicts 5.4% errors and makes 4.4%, while the other models make 2.7 to 4.8 times as many errors as their confidence scores predict [Sarvam](https://www.sarvam.ai/blogs/sarvam-prism).

On recorded agent runs, Sarvam says Prism scores a full run in about half a second on a single GPU, against a median 2.5 seconds and $0.64 per 1,000 runs for Claude Haiku 5.5 through its API, and it handles up to 131,072 tokens per question [Sarvam](https://www.sarvam.ai/blogs/sarvam-prism).

"Prism's weights are on Hugging Face under the Apache 2.0 licence," Sarvam writes on its [Sarvam blog](https://www.sarvam.ai/blogs/sarvam-prism).

The government-backed models aim at different jobs: EKA-1 was trained on nearly 7.5 trillion tokens, supports all 22 scheduled Indian languages plus English and targets mathematics, coding, reasoning and agentic use, while the Dhīr models are mixture-of-experts models trained from scratch on Google Cloud TPUs [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/tech/indiaai-mission-unveils-three-indigenous-ai-models-as-soket-launches-eka-1-and-zenteiq-releases-dhr-2b-and-5b).

*Nothing verifiable this week from Krutrim, AI4Bharat, BharatGen or the IIT and IISc labs; the researchers did not reach their pages this run.*

**My read:** Prism is the most useful Indian release in weeks because it is small in what it does, picks one option and says how sure it is, which is exactly what a routing or guardrail layer in your own app needs. Treat the benchmark table as Sarvam's homework, though, and check the Indian-language calibration on your own data before you trust it.

## Government, compute and policy

The IndiaAI Mission has lowered the bar for its next compute round from 1,000 units to 512 units at a single data centre in India, covering GPUs, TPUs and other accelerators, and the C-DAC tender seeks up to 10,000 additional units for a five-year service period [Lapaas Voice](https://lapaasvoice.com/indiaai-mission-cuts-minimum-compute-capacity-requirement-to-512-units-for-new-10000-gpu-tender).

The lower bar is still high: bidders must be Indian companies, LLPs or partnerships with average annual turnover of at least ₹120 crore across FY2023-24, FY2024-25 and FY2025-26, a Tier-III or Rated-3 facility, ISO/IEC 27001 and a technical score of at least 60 out of 100 [Lapaas Voice](https://lapaasvoice.com/indiaai-mission-cuts-minimum-compute-capacity-requirement-to-512-units-for-new-10000-gpu-tender).

Bids are due on 13 Nov and technical bids open on 14 Nov, with pricing, quantities and locations settled later in rounds on the Government e-Marketplace, and empanelment does not guarantee any contract [Lapaas Voice](https://lapaasvoice.com/indiaai-mission-cuts-minimum-compute-capacity-requirement-to-512-units-for-new-10000-gpu-tender).

For scale, MeitY said in an August 2026 parliamentary update that 15 compute providers had been empanelled across four rounds, 237 projects approved for subsidised compute and 93.18 lakh GPU hours sanctioned, and universities and academic institutions are among the intended users [Lapaas Voice](https://lapaasvoice.com/indiaai-mission-cuts-minimum-compute-capacity-requirement-to-512-units-for-new-10000-gpu-tender).

At the model launch, Krishnan said the models can be deployed in air-gapped environments for enterprise AI, and subsidised AI compute and support for research expenses helped the startups build them [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/tech/indiaai-mission-unveils-three-indigenous-ai-models-as-soket-launches-eka-1-and-zenteiq-releases-dhr-2b-and-5b).

### Compute on your desk

RTX Spark laptops are here and priced for studios, not hostels: ASUS's ProArt P14 with 24GB memory and a 1TB SSD costs Rs 3,29,990, and the ProArt P16 costs Rs 4,99,990 with 64GB and Rs 6,49,990 with 128GB of unified memory [MySmartPrice](https://www.mysmartprice.com/personal-tech/laptops-pc/laptops-pc-news/asus-proart-p16-and-p14-laptops-launched-in-india-with-nvidia-rtx-spark-n1x-prices-start-at-rs-329990/).

The P16 pairs the RTX Spark N1X platform's 6,144-core GPU with a 20-core CPU, the laptops went on sale on 3 Nov, and the launch offer includes 0% no-cost EMI from Rs 13,750 per month until 30 Nov [MySmartPrice](https://www.mysmartprice.com/personal-tech/laptops-pc/laptops-pc-news/asus-proart-p16-and-p14-laptops-launched-in-india-with-nvidia-rtx-spark-n1x-prices-start-at-rs-329990/).

**My read:** The tender wording matters more to you than the headline number, because a ₹120 crore turnover floor means the new capacity still comes from big operators, and your route in is a university or startup project, not a provider. The ProArt P14 is above a Rs 2.5 lakh ceiling before you add anything, so for now the cheaper path to big models is subsidised GPU hours, not a laptop.

Off the funding table, SEBI has cleared Razorpay's ₹5,000 Cr-₹6,000 Cr IPO, which is expected to value it at around ₹47,000 Cr with a listing likely between January and March 2027 [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/from-giva-to-eloelo-group-indian-startups-raised-255-mn-this-week/).

## Ideas for India

### A calibration check for Indian-language models

Sarvam's own tests found that rival decision models make 2.7 to 4.8 times as many errors in Indian languages as their confidence scores predict [Sarvam](https://www.sarvam.ai/blogs/sarvam-prism).

**My read:** Any company that automates a decision above a confidence threshold in Hindi or Tamil should pay to know whether that threshold means anything. This semester, build an open harness that takes any model and a labelled Indic dataset and reports expected versus actual errors per language, then run it on Prism and on the Dhīr models.

### An offline assistant on a Dhīr model

The Dhīr models support English and 10 Indian languages and can run on mobile phones without internet, and ZenteiQ released the weights with technical documentation and evaluation tools [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/tech/indiaai-mission-unveils-three-indigenous-ai-models-as-soket-launches-eka-1-and-zenteiq-releases-dhr-2b-and-5b).

**My read:** Field staff at cooperatives, NGOs and clinics with patchy signal would use a phone assistant that never needs a network. Pick one workflow, such as filling a form by voice in one language, and ship an Android build on Dhīr 2B with a measured latency number.

### A compliance check for AI money advice

YourStory notes that digital advice must remain suitable, transparent and compliant as platforms grow, and that SEBI issued an updated Master Circular for Investment Advisers in February [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/11/powerup-money-raises-10m-expand-ai-led-wealth-management-india).

**My read:** Wealthtech startups shipping AI coaches would pay for a tool that flags advice a regulator would object to. Turn the circular into a rule set, generate test questions, and score how often a chatbot's answers break the rules.
